Where these rights come from

Every right on this page was built from proposals written by the public in the maxipublic, the open online round run on Change.org. Nothing here was authored by us or by a model working on its own. Each right generalizes a group of real proposals, and its support is counted from the votes those proposals actually received.

Rights built by grouping proposals that ask for the same thing, worded in participants’ own sentences where possible, and ranked by how the least supportive party voted.

How the rights were made

An LLM grouped the proposals in each topic by what they ask for. Each group became one right, worded with a participant’s own sentence wherever one states the demand as a general rule, verified word for word against their proposal. Where no proposal stated a general rule, the wording was drafted and is marked as such. Support is counted from the votes actually cast on each group’s proposals, with nothing predicted.

The cross-party score

Each right is scored by its worst-off party, which is the lowest of the Democrat, Republican and independent shares voting in favour, so a right cannot rank highly by pleasing the majority alone. Because only 40 Republicans took part, each share is first shrunk toward 50% in proportion to how few votes that party cast, so 3 of 3 Republicans does not outrank 11 of 14. A party with fewer than 10 votes is marked with an asterisk. Every topic contributes its best right before any contributes a second.
